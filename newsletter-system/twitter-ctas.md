# Twitter & Substack Cross-Promotion System

## Goal
- Drive Twitter followers to Substack (for weekly structure)
- Drive Substack readers to Twitter (for real-time ES updates)
- Position yourself as multi-timeframe market structure analyst

---

## Content Strategy: Driving Twitter Growth via Newsletter

### In every newsletter, include:
1. **Twitter CTA at the end** (use templates above)
2. **Tease Twitter content**: "I'll be watching [X] closely this week and sharing setups on Twitter"
3. **Reference Twitter in analysis**: "I shared this pattern on Twitter earlier this week..."

### Occasionally offer exclusives:
- "Twitter followers saw this setup 2 days early"
- "I posted real-time updates on this move on Twitter"

This creates FOMO and drives newsletter readers to follow Twitter.

---

## Weekly Workflow

**Monday-Friday:**
- Post ES observations, setups, charts on Twitter (your normal activity)
- Drop bullets into weekly-observations.md

**Newsletter day (flexible):**
- Write newsletter using template + weekly observations
- Include Twitter CTA
- Post newsletter on Substack
- Promote on Twitter with tweet template
- Pin the tweet for 24 hours

**Result:** Consistent cross-promotion, growing both audiences simultaneously.
