## Newsletter Day Checklist (90 minutes)

- [ ] Review all weekly bullets (10 min)
- [ ] Pick 3-5 most important themes/sections (5 min)
- [ ] Expand bullets into 2-3 paragraphs each (40 min)
- [ ] Add "Day Trading Takeaway" bridges (15 min)
- [ ] Write opening hook + "What I'm watching" (10 min)
- [ ] Add Twitter CTA at end (2 min)
- [ ] Proofread and send (8 min)

**Total: 90 minutes**
