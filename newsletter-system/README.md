2. **`weekly-observations.md`** - Running capture doc
3. **`twitter-ctas.md`** - Cross-promotion templates

## How To Use This System

### During the Week (5-10 min/day)

Open `weekly-observations.md` and drop bullet points as you notice things:

- "High beta spread moved from -1.7 to +7.6"
- "Semis consolidating at 50-day MA, watching LRCX, TSM"
- "Added NTAP position, up $30 since entry"
- "ES range compressed to 2026 lows, low vol environment"

**Don't write full analysis yet.** Just capture the raw observations.

By the end of the week, you'll have 15-25 bullets across different categories.

---

### Newsletter Day (90 minutes total)

Use this exact workflow from `weekly-observations.md`:

1. **Review all weekly bullets** (10 min)
2. **Pick 3-5 most important themes** (5 min)
3. **Expand bullets into 2-3 paragraphs each** (40 min)
4. **Add "Day Trading Takeaway" bridges** (15 min)
5. **Write opening hook + "What I'm watching"** (10 min)
6. **Add Twitter CTA** from `twitter-ctas.md` (2 min)
7. **Proofread and send** (8 min)

**Total: 90 minutes**

You're not starting from blank page anymore—you're organizing and expanding notes you already captured.

---

### After Publishing

1. **Post to Substack**
2. **Promote on Twitter** using tweet template from `twitter-ctas.md`
3. **Pin the tweet** for 24 hours
4. **Archive your weekly observations** and start fresh

## Key Principles

### 1. Flexible Format
You don't need to use every section in the template every week. Pick 3-5 sections based on what's important.

### 2. No Fixed Schedule
Newsletter goes out weekly-ish, but you pick the day based on when you have something worth saying. Don't force it to be "every Saturday" if that creates pressure.

### 3. ES Content: Environment, Not Tactics
- **Include ES when there's structure:** breakouts, patterns, major levels
- **Keep it brief when there's not:** "ES in low vol, sitting tight, follow Twitter"
- **Never include intraday levels** that will be stale in hours

### 4. Bridge to Day Trading
After major sections (macro, sector rotation, swings), add a quick bullet:

**→ Day Trading Takeaway:** [How does this affect ES?]

This makes the connection explicit for ES traders in your audience.

### 5. Twitter Integration
Every newsletter should:
- End with Twitter CTA
- Tease what you'll be watching on Twitter this week
- Drive readers to Twitter for real-time ES updates
